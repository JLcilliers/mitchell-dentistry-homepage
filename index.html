General Dentistry in Fort Myers for Long-Term Oral Health

Great dental health starts with the basics, done right and never rushed.

Our general dentistry visits include comprehensive exams, cleanings, digital X-rays, and periodontal therapy, all with an eye toward catching small issues before they become even bigger problems.

We use tools like intraoral cameras and CariVu caries detection so you can actually see what we see and buffered local anesthetic to keep visits as comfortable as possible.

General Dentistry

Dental Implants in Fort Myers for Missing Teeth and Stronger Smiles

Missing teeth shouldn't mean missing out.

Our implant patients get guided, digitally planned surgery, implants restored right here in our office, and options ranging from a single implant-retained crown to full-arch solutions like All-on-4 and All-on-6.

With CBCT imaging and an in-house lab, we can move through treatment with more precision and fewer outside referrals along the way.

Dental Implants

Same-Day CEREC Crowns and Restorative Dentistry in Fort Myers

Most crowns here are same-day. Our CEREC technology means no temporary crown, no second appointment, and no goopy impressions. Patients who remember getting a crown the old way are usually surprised how different it is now. It's one piece of a broader restorative lineup that includes bridges, dentures, partials, and full-mouth restoration, with roughly 90% of our lab work done in-house for a faster turnaround.

Advanced Dental Technology Chosen for a Better Patient Experience

Questions Fort Myers Patients Ask About Mitchell Dentistry

Is Mitchell Dentistry accepting new patients in Fort Myers, FL?

Yes. New patients are welcome with all three doctors, and Dr. Skates in particular has open availability. Call (239) 880-2603 to book your first visit.

Does Mitchell Dentistry offer dental implants in Fort Myers?

Yes. We handle guided implant surgery, implant restorations, implant-retained dentures, and All-on-4 and All-on-6 cases, with digital treatment planning from start to finish.

Can I get a same-day dental crown at Mitchell Dentistry?

In most cases, yes. Our in-house CEREC milling lets us design, mill, and place a crown in a single visit for eligible cases. More complex cases may need additional time.

Does Mitchell Dentistry offer same-day emergency dental care?

Yes. We hold same-day slots for both new and existing patients whenever the schedule allows. Call (239) 939-5556 and you'll speak to a person.
